## Disambiguation Details

This individual is distinct from:

1. **Other Islamic scholars with similar names:** Multiple "Mustafa" names exist in Islamic scholarship; the Dakahlia Governorate birthplace and Grand Egyptian Museum controversy serve as distinguishing factors.

2. **Amer and Abdel Moneim al-Shahat:** Other Salafi clerics mentioned in the same context who issued separate controversial fatwas.

3. **Ahmad El-Samalusi:** Quran reciter who was separately arrested for reciting verses referencing Pharaoh at the Grand Egyptian Museum.

**Key distinguishing identifiers:**
- Salafi preacher/scholar from Minya Samanoud, Dakahlia
- Known for Quranic exegesis, Hadith studies, and Islamic jurisprudence
- Controversial fatwas including prohibition on women wearing high heels
- Detained November 3, 2025, following comments about the Grand Egyptian Museum
