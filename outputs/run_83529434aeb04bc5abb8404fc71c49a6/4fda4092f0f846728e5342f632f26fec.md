### **1. Al-Adawy’s Worldview: Epistemology, Authority, and Ideological Foundations**

#### **1.1 Core Beliefs and Epistemological Framework**
Al-Adawy’s ideology is rooted in **Salafi exclusivism**, a framework that prioritizes the **Salaf al-Salih (pious predecessors)** as the sole authoritative source of Islamic practice. His worldview is characterized by:

- **Scriptural Literalism**: Rejection of *taqlid* (blind following of scholarly traditions) in favor of direct Quranic and Hadith interpretation.
- **Anti-Institutionalism**: Distrust of state-aligned religious institutions (e.g., Al-Azhar), which he frames as "palace scholars" (*ulama al-sultan*).
- **Cultural Puritanism**: Opposition to pre-Islamic (*jahiliyya*) cultural practices, including Pharaonic heritage, which he labels as *shirk* (idolatry).
